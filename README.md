## akhilesh.art

### Books

PDFs and EPUBs added to `books/` show up on my CrossPoint reader. The Books workflow turns each PDF into an EPUB sized for the reader and updates the catalog at https://akhilesh.art/books/opds.xml, which is saved on the reader under Settings, Connectivity, OPDS Servers. The converter lives in my [CrossPoint fork](https://github.com/akhileshthite/crosspoint-reader/tree/develop/tools/paper-library).
